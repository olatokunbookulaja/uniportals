## Getting Started

### Creating Your Account

1. **After Admission**: You will receive an email with your registration number and temporary password
2. **First Login**: Go to https://uniportals.ng/student
3. **Enter Credentials**:
   - Username: Your registration number (e.g., STU2024/001234)
   - Password: Temporary password from email
4. **Change Password**: You'll be prompted to change your password on first login
5. **Set Security Questions**: Choose and answer 2-3 security questions

### Password Requirements

Your password must:
- Be at least 8 characters long
- Include uppercase and lowercase letters
- Include at least one number
- Include at least one special character (!@#$%^&*)

### Logging In

1. Visit https://uniportals.ng/student
2. Enter your registration number
3. Enter your password
4. Click "Login"

**Forgot Password?**
- Click "Forgot Password" on the login page
- Enter your registration number and email
- Check your email for reset instructions

## Course Registration

### When to Register

Course registration opens at the beginning of each semester. Check the academic calendar for exact dates.

### Step-by-Step Registration

#### 1. Check Your Eligibility

Before registering:
- Ensure all fees are paid
- Verify you're not on academic probation
- Check for any holds on your account

#### 2. Access Registration

1. Click "Course Registration" from dashboard
2. Select the current semester
3. View available courses for your level

#### 3. Select Your Courses

**Compulsory Courses**: Automatically selected, cannot be removed

**Elective Courses**:
1. Browse available electives
2. Check course description and credit units
3. Verify schedule doesn't conflict
4. Click "Add Course"

**Credit Unit Limits**:
- Minimum: 15 credit units
- Maximum: 24 credit units
- Maximum with permission: 30 credit units

#### 4. Review and Submit

1. Review your course list
2. Check total credit units
3. Verify course schedules
4. Click "Submit Registration"
5. Print your course form

### Late Registration

- Late registration incurs a penalty fee
- Available for 2 weeks after normal period
- Fee must be paid before registering

### Dropping a Course

To drop a course:
1. Go to "Course Registration"
2. Click "My Registered Courses"
3. Click "Drop" next to the course
4. Confirm your decision
5. Note: Only possible within first 4 weeks

### Adding a Course

To add a course after registration:
1. Submit request through "Course Registration"
2. Select "Add Course"
3. Provide reason for late addition
4. Wait for approval (usually 2-3 days)

## Academic Records

### Viewing Your Results

#### Semester Results

1. Navigate to "Academic Records"
2. Click "Results"
3. Select academic session and semester
4. View your grades and GPA

**Grade Scale**:
- A: 70-100 (4.0)
- B: 60-69 (3.5)
- C: 50-59 (3.0)
- D: 45-49 (2.5)
- E: 40-44 (2.0)
- F: 0-39 (0.0)

#### Cumulative Results

View your overall academic performance:
- CGPA (Cumulative Grade Point Average)
- Total credit units earned
- Academic standing
- Level progression status

### Downloading Documents

#### Course Registration Form
1. Go to "Course Registration"
2. Click "Print Course Form"
3. Save or print PDF

#### Result Statement
1. Go to "Academic Records"
2. Select semester
3. Click "Download Result"

#### Transcript Request
1. Navigate to "Documents"
2. Click "Request Transcript"
3. Fill application form
4. Pay transcript fee
5. Processing takes 5-7 working days

### Academic Standing

**Good Standing**: CGPA ≥ 2.5

**Academic Warning**: CGPA 2.0-2.49
- Must improve in next semester
- Limited course load

**Academic Probation**: CGPA < 2.0
- Maximum 18 credit units
- Mandatory academic counseling
- Must achieve 2.5 GPA or face withdrawal

## Payment and Fees

### Viewing Your Fees

1. Click "Fees and Payments"
2. View current invoices
3. See payment history
4. Check outstanding balance

### Making Payments

#### Online Payment (Recommended)

1. Go to "Make Payment"
2. Select invoice or amount
3. Choose payment method:
   - **Card Payment** (Paystack/Flutterwave)
   - **Bank Transfer**
   - **USSD Code**

**Card Payment Steps**:
1. Click "Pay with Card"
2. Enter card details
3. Authorize with OTP
4. Save receipt

**Bank Transfer Steps**:
1. Click "Pay with Bank Transfer"
2. Note the account details
3. Make transfer from your bank
4. Upload transfer receipt
5. Wait for confirmation (24 hours)

#### Bank Deposit

1. Print payment advice from portal
2. Visit any bank branch
3. Make deposit with payment advice
4. Keep bank teller receipt
5. Upload receipt on portal

### Payment Verification

- Payments are verified within 24 hours
- Check email for confirmation
- Refresh "Payment History" page
- Contact bursary if not confirmed after 48 hours

### Fee Structure

**Tuition Fee**: Varies by program
**Library Fee**: ₦5,000
**Medical Fee**: ₦3,000
**Sports Fee**: ₦2,000
**ICT Fee**: ₦10,000
**Student Union**: ₦1,000
**Hostel Fee**: ₦50,000 (if allocated)

### Installment Payments

Some fees may be paid in installments:
1. First installment: 60% (due at registration)
2. Second installment: 40% (due mid-semester)

### Refund Policy

Refunds are processed for:
- Withdrawal before semester starts (90% refund)
- Medical withdrawal (50% refund)
- Processing time: 4-6 weeks

## Hostel Accommodation

### Applying for Hostel

#### Eligibility
- New students (100 level)
- Students from outside the state
- Students with special needs

#### Application Process

1. Navigate to "Hostel"
2. Click "Apply for Accommodation"
3. Fill application form
4. Upload required documents:
   - Passport photograph
   - Guarantor form
   - LGA certificate (if claiming catchment)
5. Submit application
6. Pay hostel fee if approved

#### Allocation

- Allocations done by balloting
- Check "Hostel Status" for updates
- If allocated, accept within 48 hours
- Print allocation letter

### Checking Allocation

1. Go to "Hostel"
2. Click "My Allocation"
3. View hostel, room, and bed space details

### Hostel Rules

- Check-in within 2 weeks of allocation
- No overnight guests
- Quiet hours: 10 PM - 6 AM
- Keep room clean and tidy
- Report damages immediately

### Check-out Process

At end of academic year:
1. Submit check-out request
2. Room inspection scheduled
3. Clear any damages
4. Return room keys
5. Obtain clearance certificate

## Profile Management

### Updating Personal Information

1. Click on your name (top right)
2. Select "Profile"
3. Update editable fields:
   - Phone number
   - Email address
   - Contact address
   - Emergency contact
4. Save changes

**Note**: Some fields require verification:
- Name changes: Submit request to registry
- Date of birth: Requires supporting documents

### Uploading Profile Photo

1. Go to "Profile"
2. Click on current photo
3. Choose new photo
4. Photo requirements:
   - Passport size
   - White background
   - File size: Max 2MB
   - Format: JPG, PNG
5. Save

### Managing Contacts

Add emergency contacts:
1. Navigate to "Profile" > "Contacts"
2. Click "Add Contact"
3. Fill in details:
   - Name
   - Relationship
   - Phone
   - Email
   - Address
4. Save

## Security Tips

### Keep Your Account Safe

1. **Never share your password**
2. **Log out after each session**
3. **Don't use public computers for sensitive operations**
4. **Enable two-factor authentication** (if available)
5. **Review account activity regularly**
6. **Report suspicious activity immediately**

### Recognizing Phishing

Be aware of:
- Emails asking for password
- Suspicious links
- Unexpected payment requests
- Urgent requests for personal information

**Remember**: UniPortals will NEVER ask for your password via email.

## Glossary

**CGPA**: Cumulative Grade Point Average - overall academic performance

**GPA**: Grade Point Average - performance in a single semester

**Credit Unit**: Measure of course workload (typically 1 unit = 1 hour lecture/week)

**Semester**: Academic period (usually 16 weeks)

**Session**: Academic year (2 semesters)

**Level**: Year of study (100, 200, 300, 400, 500)

**Elective**: Optional course you can choose

**Compulsory/Core**: Required course you must take

**Prerequisite**: Course that must be completed before taking another

**Carryover**: Failed course to be repeated
